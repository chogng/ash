# Design workflow

Use this workflow for visual, interaction, product, brand, and creative design work. Work from the user's brief, intended audience, existing assets, and target medium.

## Understand the design constraints

Inspect the supplied design system, reference, template, or existing product before choosing a direction. Preserve explicit style, content, accessibility, platform, and format requirements. Ask about an unknown that would change the direction materially; otherwise make ordinary design decisions and state significant assumptions.

Choose a coherent visual and interaction direction suited to the request. Do not impose a universal aesthetic, fashionable component, color palette, or technology stack. Explain important design choices in terms of the audience, use, and constraints.

## Make the appropriate result

Deliver the requested form: concepts for a conceptual discussion, visual assets for an asset request, a mockup for a mockup, and a working interface for an implementation request. Use the actual design, media, artifact, and coding tools suitable for that form.

Develop hierarchy, typography, color, spacing, layout, content, and interaction as a coherent system. Match the established product conventions where relevant. Use a supporting coding or cowork workflow when implementation or document production is necessary.

Keep internal tool or model details out of the designed user flow unless they help its audience make a meaningful decision. Distinguish a demonstrated interaction from one actually connected to working behavior.

## Verify the target experience

Inspect the output in the intended medium using available previews or renderers. Check legibility, content, alignment, clipping, contrast, and required states. For an interactive interface, verify relevant behavior, input, navigation, and required responsive or accessibility conditions.

Use references for factual or sourced content where needed. Do not claim usability, accessibility, functionality, or production readiness beyond what was checked.

Deliver the supported editable source or final asset requested by the user, with a usable preview or reference. Explain only the design decisions and limitations necessary to assess and use it. Publishing or deployment follows the shared authorization rules.
